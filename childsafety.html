Child Safety Standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation

Earthafying is a calm, photo-first social space built on kindness and safety. The protection of children is a non-negotiable priority for us. This page sets out the standards we hold ourselves to, and how anyone can report a concern.

Zero tolerance. Earthafying has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content, account, or behaviour of this nature is prohibited and will be removed and reported.

Who can use Earthafying

  • Earthafying is for people aged 13 and over. We apply an age gate at sign-up.
  • Earthafying has no private messaging of any kind. There are no direct messages, no private chat, and no way for one user to contact another privately. Everything shared is public and in the open.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • AI moderation on all content. Every photo and every piece of text posted to Earthafying is checked by automated moderation before and after it appears.
  • In-app reporting. Any signed-in member can report a post directly inside the app. Reports reach us immediately for review.
  • Muting. Members can mute accounts they do not wish to see.
  • No private channels to groom or isolate a child. Because there is no private messaging, the platform does not offer a route for grooming or private contact.

How to report a child safety concern

If you see content or behaviour that may involve the safety of a child, please tell us straight away:

  • In the app: use the Report button on any post.
  • By email: contact hello@earthafying.com.

Our designated point of contact reviews child safety reports and is able to respond to enquiries about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.

How we respond

  • We review reports promptly and remove violating content and accounts.
  • We act to comply with all relevant child safety laws in the regions where Earthafying is available.
  • Where required, we report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate regional and national authorities, including the relevant reporting bodies.

Contact

Point of contact for child safety matters: hello@earthafying.com
